\section{Conclusion}
On 40 matched families, intent-aware editing recorded 22/40 strict successes, compared with 24/40 for local editing and 21/40 for instruction consolidation. The guarded variant recorded 23/40. The differences reflect the combined effects of state presentation and plan editing, along with the behavior of the shared model. The checkpoint design reveals withdrawn effects that revised final-state scoring can miss. The comparison applies to these recoverable interruptions and the specific predicates and model.
