Agents that receive revised goals during tool use must distinguish revoked actions, retained work, and completed effects. We evaluate matched AppWorld continuations before a verified withdrawn write. Across 40 historical families, tolerant local validation reduced full fallback from 19/40 to 0/40, while strict success was 22/40 for both intent-aware revisions (paired difference +0.0 points, 95\% bootstrap interval [-7.5, 7.5]). The guarded method blocked 0 of 186 checked historical requests and had 21/40 strict successes. A separate confirmation cohort included 9 unused families. An independent scoring audit identified compatibility and instruction conflicts. The study separates structural reliability from task completion, and guard detection from the opportunity to enforce it.
