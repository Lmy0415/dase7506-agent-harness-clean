A mid-task revision can arrive before a tool request commits or after its effects persist. We first analyze 405 historical AppWorld branches across 49 task families. More tolerant intent and evidence validation reduces complete fallback from 19/40 to 0/40 in the historical cohort, with strict success unchanged at 22/40. We then run 72 branches in matched PRE and POST worlds around a verified tool submission, reusing 12 historical families. The PACT controller can report effects and seek authorization automatically while scheduling independent work. POST Appropriate Resolution Rate is 7/12 for PACT, 2/12 for the full procedural prompt, and 2/12 for native continuation. The observed paired difference is +41.7 [16.7, 66.7] percentage points (estimate and 95\% bootstrap interval). The results describe complete harnesses in a small matched sample.
